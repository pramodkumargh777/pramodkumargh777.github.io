01Context

Stock you can't see is stock you can't plan.

A manufacturing inventory platform needed live stock visibility to improve planning. Data arrived in real time over MQTT and was served to planning through REST APIs.

I built the APIs and ingestion workflows in Python, Flask and PostgreSQL, and automated how they shipped.

02Architecture

Ingest in real time, serve from one source of truth.

03Performance

Most performance problems end in the database.

API response times dropped by 30% through query optimisation and indexing.

04Delivery
